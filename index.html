How scoring works

  • A correct answer is worth 1000 points at the first frame, dropping to 100 once fully zoomed out.
  • A wrong answer costs 150 and locks you out for the rest of that round. Everyone else keeps playing.
  • After buzzing you have 12 seconds to type the animal's name and press Enter. Small typos and common nicknames like "hippo" count.
  • Type the animal's exact name (spelled right, as shown on the reveal) for double points. Capitals, spaces and punctuation don't matter.
  • Buzz with your key or tap your button. Space moves to the next animal.
